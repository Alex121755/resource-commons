Field case / School resources

The teachers’ office

The Resource Commons team uses the project to manage resources in a teachers’ office. Staff can record where an item belongs and choose which colleagues can see an offer.

Open groups →

Example workflow

From shelf to staff group

  1. Record the item

    For example, a teacher puts a spare whiteboard on the office shelf. A photo and the last confirmed location help the next person recognize it.

  2. Choose the audience

    The teacher creates or joins the staff group, then posts the whiteboard there. Only group members can see the shared listing.

  3. Arrange the handoff

    A colleague checks the listing and agrees on pickup and return with the owner. After the return, the teacher can update the inventory location.
